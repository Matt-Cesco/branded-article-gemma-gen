# Audience Definition

## Primary Audience

The primary audience is mostly aged 50+ and may include people who:

- Have accessibility requirements.
- Have reduced mobility.
- Use a wheelchair.
- Use a mobility scooter.
- Use walking aids.
- Travel with a partner.
- Travel with family.
- Travel with a carer.
- Require additional care or assistance.
- Have several accessibility requirements at the same time.

Do not treat this as one homogeneous audience. Two wheelchair users may need very different room layouts, transfer support, transport arrangements or excursion options. A person with reduced mobility may not identify as disabled. A partner or adult child may be researching on behalf of someone else.

## Possible Concerns

Readers may be asking:

- Will the hotel room work for me?
- Is there step-free access?
- Will the bathroom meet my requirements?
- Can I take my mobility scooter?
- What happens at the airport?
- Will transport work with my wheelchair?
- Can I participate in excursions?
- What happens if I need help?
- Is someone available to discuss my requirements?
- Will my partner or carer be accommodated?
- How much assistance can be arranged?
- Can someone check the details for me?
- Will I feel comfortable and included?
- Will the journey be tiring?
- Is the destination practical for my level of mobility?
- Can equipment be stored, charged or transported?
- What details should I confirm before booking?

## Information Needs

Readers need:

- Plain answers to the search question.
- Clear explanations of accessibility considerations.
- Practical details, not generic reassurance.
- What to check before booking.
- What may vary by hotel, room type, transport provider, ship, airline, destination or excursion.
- Which details must be personally confirmed.
- How Limitless can help.
- What happens if they call.
- Links to relevant holidays, destinations or further guidance.

## Emotional Needs

Readers may need to feel:

- Understood.
- Respected.
- Not rushed.
- Not judged.
- Less uncertain.
- More confident asking questions.
- Able to discuss personal requirements without embarrassment.
- Excited about travel as well as reassured about practicalities.

Empathy should be calm and practical. Do not exaggerate anxiety or frame the reader as helpless.

## Cognitive Considerations

Because many users are 50+, optimise for:

- Clear language.
- Short paragraphs.
- Descriptive headings.
- Strong hierarchy.
- No unnecessary abbreviations.
- No excessive jargon.
- Predictable structure.
- Easy scanning.
- Explicit next steps.

Do not patronise older users. Age should influence clarity, not intelligence assumptions.

## Reader Segments

### The Traveller Researching For Themselves

They may know exactly what they need and want specific information. Respect their expertise in their own requirements.

### The Partner Or Family Researcher

They may be trying to understand what to ask, what to check and how to avoid choosing the wrong option. Give them decision support without assuming they are the decision-maker.

### The Carer Or Support Person

They may need practical details about care, transfers, equipment, transport and daily support. Avoid making care promises unless verified.

### The Returning Traveller

They may know accessible travel is possible and want a suitable destination, cruise or holiday type. Keep the content efficient and commercially useful.

### The Nervous First-Time Accessible Travel Researcher

They may need reassurance and plain explanations. Answer basic questions without talking down to them.
