# Internal Linking

Internal links should help the reader continue their decision journey. They should not be added merely for SEO.

## Possible Link Targets

- Destination page.
- Relevant holiday.
- Holiday category.
- Related article.
- FAQ.
- Accessibility information.
- Enquiry or call path.

## Typical Informational Funnel

Article -> destination/category -> holiday -> phone enquiry.

## Anchor Text Rules

Anchor text must be descriptive.

Use:

- "accessible holidays in Tenerife"
- "wheelchair accessible cruises"
- "mobility scooter travel advice"
- "speak with our travel team"

Avoid:

- "click here"
- "read more"
- "learn more"
- "this page"

Use "click here" only when unavoidable, which should be rare.

## Link Placement

- Add links where the reader has a natural next question.
- Do not overload paragraphs with links.
- Avoid placing several links next to one another.
- Use conversion links after relevance has been established.
- Use informational links when the reader needs more explanation.

## Do Not Send Users Backwards In Intent

If a reader is close to enquiring about a specific accessible holiday, do not send them to a broad beginner guide unless it answers a real concern.

If a reader is reading a basic informational article, do not push them straight to a narrow holiday page before they understand the topic.

## Distinguish Link Types

Informational links help the reader understand.

Commercial links help the reader explore options.

Conversion links invite a conversation.

Each article should include a balanced link path that matches intent.

## Link Quality Checklist

- Does the link help the reader?
- Is the anchor descriptive?
- Does the link match the reader's current stage?
- Is the linked page likely to answer the next question?
- Is the link placed in a useful sentence?
- Would removing the link make the article less helpful?
