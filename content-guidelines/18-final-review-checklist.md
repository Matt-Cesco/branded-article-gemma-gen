# Final Publication Checklist

Use this checklist before publication.

## Search Intent

- [ ] Does the first section actually answer the search query?
- [ ] Is the article format appropriate for the intent?
- [ ] Has the reader's main question been answered before any strong CTA?
- [ ] Does the article avoid drifting into generic travel copy?

## SEO

- [ ] Is the main keyword used naturally?
- [ ] Are secondary keywords used only where helpful?
- [ ] Are headings descriptive?
- [ ] Is there no keyword stuffing?
- [ ] Is the article as long as needed, without filler?
- [ ] Has cannibalisation with existing content been considered?

## Helpfulness

- [ ] Does the article provide useful practical information?
- [ ] Does it answer likely follow-up questions?
- [ ] Are checklists, examples or decision points included where useful?
- [ ] Does the reader know what to do next?

## Accessibility

- [ ] Could any accessibility statement be interpreted as a guarantee?
- [ ] Has any accessibility feature been invented?
- [ ] Does the article acknowledge individual requirements?
- [ ] Does it avoid describing accessibility as a simple yes/no where nuance is needed?
- [ ] Are room, transport, equipment, care and excursion claims verified or caveated?
- [ ] Is "fully accessible" avoided unless precisely verified?

## Factual Accuracy

- [ ] Are facts that may change flagged for checking?
- [ ] Are destination, airline, hotel, cruise or transport claims current?
- [ ] Are unsupported claims removed?
- [ ] Are external facts sourced where needed?

## Tone

- [ ] Does the article sound warm without becoming patronising?
- [ ] Does it avoid pity, inspiration clichés and exaggerated positivity?
- [ ] Does it use British English?
- [ ] Does it avoid corporate jargon?

## Readability

- [ ] Are paragraphs easy to read?
- [ ] Are headings descriptive?
- [ ] Is jargon explained?
- [ ] Is the structure predictable and easy to scan?
- [ ] Are older readers considered through clarity, not patronising simplification?

## Empathy

- [ ] Are likely concerns acknowledged respectfully?
- [ ] Does the article avoid dramatizing accessibility challenges?
- [ ] Does it respect the reader's knowledge of their own requirements?

## Trust

- [ ] Does the reader understand how Limitless can help?
- [ ] Has trust been established before the strongest CTA?
- [ ] Are reviews or testimonials used only if supplied?
- [ ] Are expectations set honestly?

## Conversion

- [ ] Is there one clear primary conversion path?
- [ ] Does the CTA match search intent?
- [ ] Is the CTA contextual to the article?
- [ ] Does the article avoid aggressive urgency?

## Phone CTA

- [ ] Is there a clear reason to call rather than simply "Contact us"?
- [ ] Does the CTA mention individual requirements where relevant?
- [ ] Does it explain the value of speaking with the team?
- [ ] Is the CTA placed after enough usefulness and trust?

## Internal Links

- [ ] Are internal links genuinely useful?
- [ ] Is anchor text descriptive?
- [ ] Do links support the reader's decision journey?
- [ ] Are paragraphs not overloaded with links?

## Metadata

- [ ] Is the SEO title accurate?
- [ ] Is the meta description helpful and not overpromising?
- [ ] Is the slug concise and readable?
- [ ] Does metadata avoid unsupported accessibility guarantees?

## Human Review

- [ ] Could a human editor comfortably publish this?
- [ ] Has factual review been completed?
- [ ] Has accessibility review been completed?
- [ ] Have all unknowns been resolved or clearly flagged?
