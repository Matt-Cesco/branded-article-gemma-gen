# Article Structure

Use the standard Limitless SEO article architecture unless the search intent requires a variation.

## A. SEO Metadata

Generate:

- SEO title.
- Meta description.
- Slug.
- H1.
- Primary keyword.
- Secondary keywords.
- Search intent.
- Related user questions.

Metadata should be clear, specific and accurate. Do not use "fully accessible" unless verified and precisely scoped.

## B. Opening / Answer

The first 80-150 words must:

- Acknowledge the actual search question.
- Answer it quickly.
- Avoid generic travel introductions.
- Create relevance.
- Introduce reassurance only where justified.
- Tell the user what they will learn.

Good opening pattern:

> "Yes, you can often take a mobility scooter on a plane, but the rules depend on the airline, battery type, size and weight. You will usually need to give details in advance and arrange airport assistance if required. This guide explains what to check before you fly and when it may help to speak with a travel specialist."

## C. Immediate Answer

If the title contains a question, give the answer near the beginning. Do not force the reader to scroll 700 words to obtain the answer.

## D. Educational Content

Use logical H2 and H3 sections.

Explain:

- What the subject means.
- Practical considerations.
- Accessibility considerations.
- Preparation.
- Available choices.
- Possible limitations.
- Questions worth asking.

## E. Accessibility Suitability

Have a dedicated section answering:

> "What should someone with accessibility requirements consider?"

This section should translate generic travel information into practical accessibility decision-making.

Include where relevant:

- Room layout.
- Bathroom setup.
- Step-free routes.
- Transport.
- Transfers.
- Equipment.
- Excursions.
- Care or support.
- Distance, gradients and surfaces.
- What must be checked before booking.

## F. Limitless Expertise

Naturally introduce how Limitless may help. Do not suddenly change from editorial content into advertising.

Good transition:

> "Because these details can vary by traveller, hotel and departure date, it can help to talk them through before choosing a holiday."

## G. Reassurance

Answer likely remaining objections. Use evidence wherever possible.

Reassurance should say what can be checked, explained or discussed. Do not guarantee outcomes without evidence.

## H. Relevant Holiday Or Destination Connection

Where appropriate, connect information with relevant Limitless offerings:

- Destination pages.
- Holiday category pages.
- Relevant holiday pages.
- Related accessible travel guides.

Only link where it helps the reader continue their journey.

## I. Phone CTA

Place the strongest phone CTA only after sufficient relevance and trust.

The CTA should explain why calling helps:

> "If you are unsure whether this destination will work with your wheelchair, mobility scooter or care requirements, speak with our travel team. We can talk through the details that matter to you and help you explore suitable options."

## J. FAQ

Typically include 3-7 genuinely useful questions.

FAQ answers should:

- Be concise.
- Avoid invented facts.
- Reinforce individual suitability.
- Link only when useful.

## K. Closing

The closing should:

- Summarise the practical answer.
- Reassure.
- Give one clear next step.

## Structure Variations By Intent

Informational articles should answer first and delay stronger CTAs.

Problem/question articles should explain the process before introducing Limitless.

Destination research articles should balance destination appeal with realistic accessibility considerations.

Commercial research articles should help compare options and define suitability criteria.

High commercial intent articles may introduce relevant Limitless options earlier, but must still provide useful information and accessibility caveats.
