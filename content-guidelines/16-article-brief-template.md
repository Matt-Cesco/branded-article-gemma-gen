# Article Brief Template

Copy this structure when creating a new article brief.

```yaml
---
article_type:
topic:
primary_keyword:
secondary_keywords: []
search_intent:
funnel_stage:
target_reader:
target_country: UK
language: en-GB

conversion:
  primary_goal: phone_call
  cta_strength:
  relevant_phone_context:

seo:
  suggested_title:
  suggested_slug:
  meta_description:
  related_queries: []
  questions_to_answer: []

limitless:
  relevant_destinations: []
  relevant_holidays: []
  relevant_services: []
  verified_accessibility_information: []

content:
  target_length:
  mandatory_sections: []
  optional_sections: []
  faq: true

trust:
  available_reviews: []
  available_evidence: []

fact_checking:
  external_research_required:
  unknown_information: []
---
```

## Search Intent Analysis

State the likely intent category from `06-search-intent.md`. Explain what the reader wants answered first and what would count as a poor intent match.

## Reader Need

Describe the practical information the reader needs to make progress.

## Reader Anxiety Or Uncertainty

List the concerns the article should reduce. Include accessibility, journey, accommodation, transport, care or support concerns where relevant.

## Primary Answer

Write the short answer the article must provide near the beginning.

## Content Angle

Define the editorial angle. It should be specific enough to prevent generic travel writing.

## Accessibility Angle

Explain the accessibility suitability question the article must address. Identify any features or claims that require verification.

## Limitless Angle

Explain how Limitless may help without forcing sales copy into the opening.

## Conversion Strategy

Define:

- CTA strength.
- CTA timing.
- Reason to call.
- Relevant phone context.
- Any softer earlier conversion points.

## Heading Structure

Provide a proposed H1, H2s and H3s. Headings should be descriptive and should follow the reader's decision journey.

## Internal Links

List intended internal links and why each helps the user.

## Evidence Needed

List facts that must be verified before publication, especially accessibility claims.

## CTA Plan

Specify the CTA category from `09-phone-conversion.md` or `12-cta-library.md`, plus draft CTA copy.

## FAQ Opportunities

List 3-7 genuine questions. Avoid creating FAQs solely for keyword variations.

## Things Not To Claim

List prohibited or unsupported claims for this article, such as:

- Fully accessible.
- Suitable for everyone.
- Specific room features without verification.
- Specific transport arrangements without verification.
- Care support without verification.
- Reviews or testimonials not supplied.
