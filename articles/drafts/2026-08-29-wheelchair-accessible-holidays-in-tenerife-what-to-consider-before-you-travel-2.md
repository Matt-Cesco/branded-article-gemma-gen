# Wheelchair Accessible Holidays in Tenerife: What to Consider Before You Travel

Tenerife has long been a favoured destination for those seeking the warmth of the Canary Islands. With its stunning coastline, varied landscapes, and established tourism infrastructure, it offers a wealth of experiences. However, for those requiring wheelchair accessible holidays in Tenerife, the journey involves more than just booking a flight and a hotel.

When mobility is a factor, the definition of "accessible" can vary significantly. A room that is accessible for a person using a manual wheelchair may not necessarily provide enough turning space for a large powered wheelchair or a mobility scooter. Understanding these nuances early in your planning process is the key to ensuring your holiday in Tenerife is as relaxing and enjoyable as possible.

## Understanding Your Specific Requirements

Before looking at specific resorts or hotels, it is helpful to define what "accessible" means for you. Accessibility is deeply personal; what provides independence for one person might present a barrier for another.

Consider these factors:
*   **Your Equipment:** Are you using a manual wheelchair, a powered wheelchair, or a mobility scooter? Each requires different amounts of space and different storage considerations.
*   **Your Personal Mobility:** Do you require step-free access at all times, or do you have a limited range of mobility?
*   **Your Companions:** If you are travelling with a carer or a partner, will they need specific facilities or a layout that accommodates both of you comfortably?

By identifying these needs early, you can move away from general "accessible" labels and towards a holiday that genuinely fits your lifestyle.

## Accommodation: Looking Beyond the Label

Finding the right accommodation is often the most important part of planning accessible holidays in Tenerife. While many hotels offer "accessible rooms," it is vital to look at the specific details of those rooms.

### Bathroom Facilities
One of the most common concerns for wheelchair users is the bathroom. A standard "accessible" room may not include a wet room or a walk-in shower, which can be essential for those with limited mobility. 

When inquiring about accessible hotels in Tenerife, specifically ask:
*   Is there a walk-in shower or a wet room?
*   Are there grab rails installed in both the bathroom and the changing area?
*   Is there enough space to maneuver a wheelchair inside the bathroom?

### Room Layout and Space
If you are travelling with a mobility scooter, space is a critical factor. These devices have a larger footprint than manual wheelchairs. You will need to confirm that the room has enough floor space for both your equipment and your belongings, and that there are no "lip" thresholds at the doorways that could hinder movement.

### Location within the Resort
Sometimes a hotel is accessible, but the specific room isn't. For example, some accessible rooms might be located near the back of a complex, requiring a long journey to reach the main pool or dining areas. Identifying a "step-free" route throughout the entire resort is a key consideration for a stress-free holiday.

## Navigating Transport and Logistics

Getting to and around Tenerife involves several stages, each with its own set of accessibility considerations.

### Airport Assistance
Tenerife's airports (both North and South) have established procedures for passengers requiring assistance. However, it is important to book this assistance in advance. This ensures that staff are ready to help you navigate the terminal and reach your gate.

### Airport Transfers
Once you land, the journey to your accommodation should be seamless. Not all taxis or shuttle buses are equipped to accommodate mobility scooters or large powered wheelchairs. When booking accessible travel in Tenerife, it is often advisable to arrange a dedicated, pre-booked transport service that guarantees a vehicle with a ramp or enough space for your specific equipment.

### Local Transport
Tenerife has a decent public transport system, but navigating local buses with a wheelchair can sometimes be challenging depending on the route and the specific vehicle. If you plan to explore the island independently, it is worth researching the specific routes and locations you intend to visit.

## Exploring the Island: Beaches and Excursions

One of the primary reasons people choose Tenerife is the beautiful coastline. However, beach access can vary greatly.

### Beach Access
Not every beach in Tenerife is equally accessible. Some may have ramps and "blue flag" status, while others may be reached via stairs or uneven terrain. If a beach walk is a priority, it is important to confirm the specific access points for the beaches closest to your accommodation.
