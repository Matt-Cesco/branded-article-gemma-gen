# Wheelchair Accessible Holidays in Tenerife: What to Consider Before You Travel

Tenerife is one of the most popular destinations in the Canary Islands, known for its stunning coastline, diverse landscapes, and warm Mediterranean climate. For many, it represents the perfect retreat. However, when planning a trip with a wheelchair or reduced mobility, the "perfect" holiday depends entirely on the nuances of your individual needs.

While Tenerife offers a wide range of facilities, "accessible" is not a one-size-fits-all term. What works for a manual wheelchair user may differ significantly from the requirements of someone using a large powered mobility scooter.

To help you plan with confidence, we have outlined the key areas to consider when booking your accessible holiday in Tenerife.

## Understanding Accommodation Requirements

When booking accessible hotels or apartments in Tenerife, the most important step is moving beyond a general "accessible" label. It is vital to understand exactly what that means for your specific mobility requirements.

### Room Layout and Space
For those using powered wheelchairs or mobility scooters, space is a critical factor. You will need to consider:
*   **Turning Circles:** Is there enough room to turn a larger scooter around comfortably?
*   **Door Widths:** Are the doorways wide enough to accommodate your specific equipment without scraping?
*   **Ground Floor Access:** While many hotels have lifts, a ground-floor room can often provide easier access to dining areas and hotel amenities.

### Bathroom Facilities
The bathroom is often the most critical component of an accessible room. It is worth clarifying:
*   **Wet Rooms:** Are there step-free showers (wet rooms) available, or is there a lip to step over?
*   **Grab Rails:** Are there rails installed in the shower and by the toilet?
*   **Changing Space:** Is there enough floor space to manoeuvre and move safely within the bathroom?

## Navigating Transport in Tenerife

Getting from the airport to your accommodation, and then moving around the island, requires careful consideration.

### Airport Assistance and Transfers
Tenerife’s airports (TFN and TFS) offer assistance services, but these must be arranged in advance. When booking your transfer, it is important to confirm:
*   **Vehicle Suitability:** Can the vehicle accommodate a folded or unfolded wheelchair/scouter?
*   **Driver Knowledge:** Does the driver understand your specific needs, such as the need for extra time or specific boarding procedures?

### Local Transport
Once in Tenerife, you may choose to use local taxis or buses. While many modern taxis are equipped to assist, it is often more reliable to arrange a dedicated, pre-booked accessible transfer if you plan on visiting different locations during your stay.

## Exploring the Island: Beaches and Excursions

One of the highlights of a Tenerife holiday is exploring the local sights. However, accessibility can vary significantly depending on the location.

### Beach Access
Many beaches in Tenerife have designated "accessible" zones with ramps and adapted facilities. However, these may be located in specific areas. If a particular beach is your priority, it is worth checking the specific facilities available at that location before you arrive.

### Excursions and Sightseeing
Whether you wish to visit the Loro Parque, enjoy a boat trip, or tour the local villages, it is helpful to check the terrain of the specific excursion. Some areas may involve uneven paving or inclines that are more manageable with a companion or specific equipment.

## Planning for Your Companion
If you are travelling with a partner, family member, or carer, their comfort is equally important. Ensure that the holiday package provides enough space and facilities so that your companion can also enjoy the trip comfortably.

## How to Ensure Your Holiday is the Right Fit

We understand that there is a lot to consider, and it can feel overwhelming to ensure every detail—from the width of a bathroom door to the type of beach ramp—is correct before you commit.

The most effective way to gain peace of mind is to speak with a specialist. Rather than relying on generic descriptions, a conversation with an accessible travel expert allows you to share your specific requirements. They can help clarify the nuances of local transport, the specific layout of holiday complexes, and the practicalities of taking mobility equipment abroad.

By discussing your needs early, you can move from "hoping" the holiday is accessible to "knowing" it is.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I take my mobility scooter to Tenerife?**
Yes, you can generally take mobility scooters to Tenerife, but they must comply with airline regulations regarding size and weight. It is essential to check with your airline and your mobility provider ahead of time to ensure your specific model is permitted and how it should be packed.

**Are there accessible beaches in Tenerife?**
Many beaches in Tenerife have specific areas designed for people with reduced mobility, often featuring ramps and nearby facilities. However, because accessibility can vary, we recommend confirming the specific facilities of a beach before you visit.

**Is the hoteler and local transport in Tenerife wheelchair friendly?**
Many hotels in Tenerife are equipped with lifts and accessible rooms. Similarly, there are options for accessible transport. However, because "accessible" can mean different things to different people, it is always best to confirm the specific requirements of your chosen hotel and transport provider.

**Do I need to book airport assistance in advance?**
Yes, if you require assistance at Tenerife airport, you should contact your airline or the airport operator in advance to ensure the necessary staff and equipment are available.
