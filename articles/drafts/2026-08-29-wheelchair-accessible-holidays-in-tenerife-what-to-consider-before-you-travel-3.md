# Wheelchair Accessible Holidays in Tenerife: What to Consider Before You Travel

Tenerife is one of the most popular destinations in the Canary Islands, known for its diverse landscapes, warm climate, and high-quality tourist infrastructure. For many travellers with reduced mobility or those who use wheelchairs or mobility scooters, the island offers a wealth of accessible opportunities.

However, because every individual’s needs are unique, "accessible" can mean different things depending on your specific requirements. Whether you are looking for a relaxing beach holiday, a cultural tour, or a sunny retreat in the mountains, planning ahead is the key to ensuring your stay in Tenerife is as comfortable and enjoyable as possible.

## Understanding your specific requirements
Before booking a trip to Tenerife, it is helpful to define exactly what features are essential for your comfort and independence. Accessibility is rarely a "one size fits all" experience. 

For example, a room might be listed as "accessible" because it has a grab rail, but that may not be sufficient if you require a wide enough turning circle for a powered wheelchair or a walk-in wet room. Similarly, while some beaches may have nearby parking, not all may have easy, step-free access to the sand. 

Identifying these specific needs early allows you to ask the right questions of your travel provider and accommodation host.

## Accessible Accommodation in Tenerife
Tenerife hosts a wide range of accommodation, from large resorts to boutique hotels and private villas. When selecting a holiday, consider the following:

*   **Room Layout and Space:** If you use a mobility scooter or a large powered wheelchair, you will need sufficient space to maneuver and park your equipment within the room.
*   **Bathroom Facilities:** This is often a primary concern. Ensure the bathroom has a level entry, a wet-room style shower, and appropriate grab rails.
*   **Step-Free Access:** While the main entrance to a hotel might be flat, some interior areas or dining rooms may have small steps. Confirming a step-free route throughout the premises can make a significant difference to your independence.
*   **Storage and Charging:** If you travel with a mobility scooter, you will need a designated space to store it and a nearby power source to keep the battery charged.

## Navigating Tenerife: Transport and Logistics
Getting from the airport to your accommodation and moving around the island are vital components of your holiday.

**Airport Assistance and Transfers**
Tenerife North and South airports offer assistance services for passengers with reduced mobility. It is vital to book this in advance with your airline to ensure staff are available to help you through the terminal and to your gate.

Furthermore, when booking a transfer from the airport to your hotel, specifically request a vehicle equipped to accommodate a wheelchair or mobility scooter. Some standard taxis may not have the space or the ramp access required, so a pre-booked, accessible transfer is often the most reliable option.

**Local Transport**
Tenerife has a decent public transport network, but for those with limited mobility, pre-arranged transport can offer more peace of mind. It is worth checking the accessibility of local buses or taxis if you plan to explore the island independently.

## Beaches, Sightseeing, and Excursions
One of the main draws of Tenerife is its stunning coastline and scenic views. Many beaches in Tenerife have been adapted with walkways and ramps to make them accessible for wheelchair users. However, these features can vary significantly between different beaches.

When planning excursions, such as visiting the Loro Parque or Siam Park, it is advisable to check their specific accessibility guides. Most major attractions in Tenerife are well-equipped, but some remote hiking trails or older coastal paths may not be suitable for those with limited mobility.

## Preparing for your trip
To ensure a smooth journey, it is often helpful to compile a checklist of questions for your chosen accommodation or tour operator:
1. Is there a lift to all dining areas?
2. Can a mobility scooter be kept in the room?
3. Is the bathroom a wet-room?
4. Is there a dedicated parking space near the entrance?
5. Are there any steps between the lobby and the beach area?

## Planning with confidence
Planning a holiday when you have specific accessibility needs can sometimes feel overwhelming. There are many moving parts—from flight assistance to the specific dimensions of a shower—and it can be difficult to know if a "standard" accessible description meets your personal requirements.

If you find yourself unsure about whether a particular hotel in Tenerife will truly meet your needs, or if you have concerns about transport and mobility equipment, speaking to a specialist can provide clarity. A dedicated travel advisor can help you navigate these details, asking the specific questions on your behalf to ensure the holiday you book is the one you were hoping for.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do airports in Tenerife provide assistance for wheelchair users?**
Yes, both Tenerife North and South airports offer assistance for passengers with reduced mobility. However, you must notify your airline in advance so they can arrange for staff to assist you with navigating the airport and boarding your flight.

**Can I take a mobility scooter on a plane to Tenerife?**
Most airlines allow mobility scooters, but they must meet specific size and battery requirements. It is essential to check with your airline early in the booking process to ensure your specific equipment is approved and that you have enough space on the aircraft.

**Are most beaches in Tenerife accessible?**
Many popular beaches have been adapted with accessible paths and facilities. However, accessibility varies by location. It is always best to confirm the specific beach's facilities before you travel.

**Is there step-free access in most Tenerife hotels?**
While many modern hotels offer step-free access, older buildings may have architectural limitations. It is important to confirm the layout of the specific hotel you are booking to ensure your route to the beach or dining areas is clear.

**Are there accessible tours and excursions in Tenerife?**
Many major theme parks and attractions in Tenerife are designed to be accessible. However, some smaller tours or rural excursions may have different levels of accessibility. We recommend checking individual attraction websites or speaking with a travel specialist to confirm.
